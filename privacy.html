Privacy

Short version: nothing leaves your browser unless you choose to join the group, and you can leave the group whenever you want.

Who is responsible

Compass is a personal project by Teun, a student in the Netherlands. Questions or requests: send a message on X to @Teunemansvb.

Taking the quiz

Your answers are kept only in your own browser (in its local storage), so you can continue later. They are not sent anywhere. This site uses no cookies, no analytics and no trackers, and its fonts are hosted on the site itself.

Joining the group compass

Only if you tick the consent box and press "Add me", your browser sends to a Google Form that Teun owns:

  • your result code, which contains the name you entered, your optional label and all your answers;
  • a random removal key, so a later removal request can be matched to your result;
  • a note of what you agreed to, and when.

Teun checks new results by hand and adds them to this site. From then on, anyone who opens the site can see your name, your position on the compass and your individual answers. Use a nickname if you'd rather not be recognisable.

Why this needs your explicit consent

Your answers reveal your political opinions. Under the GDPR, that is a special category of personal data, which may only be processed with your explicit consent (Article 9(2)(a)). That is the consent box. You must be 16 or older to give it.

Who else handles the data

  • Google stores the form responses in Teun's Google account. Google may process them in the United States, under the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.
  • GitHub hosts this site. Like any web host, it keeps technical logs, such as IP addresses, when you visit.
  • Everyone who visits the site can see the results in the group.

How long it's kept

Until you ask for removal, or until Teun ends the project. When the project ends, the group list and the form responses are deleted.

Your rights

  • Withdraw your consent and be removed at any time: use "Remove my result" at the bottom of the main page, or send Teun a message on X. Your result is taken off the site and deleted from the form responses, normally within a few days. Older versions of the site's code on GitHub are cleaned up too.
  • You can also ask which data Teun has about you, or ask for a correction.
  • Once your result has been public, others may have seen or copied it; removal can't undo that.
  • If you think your data is handled wrongly, you can complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).
